STORY

THE ESTABLISHED HISTORY OF THE GAME.

The Game is an ongoing multiplayer event taking place on an island.

During Halloween 2025, the players encountered the Hallow King. No deeper lore from that event is currently established.

During Easter 2026, the Killer Bunny entered the island through the Fountain and targeted the Forest Beacon.

During Summer 2026, the Crimson Lord appeared in the Crimson Forest. The seven Seal Statues were destroyed throughout the Summer storyline.

THE SEAL SYSTEM IS GONE.

The Crimson Lord woke the Hallow King.

During Halloween 2026, the Hallow King is already awake. His objective is not to escape the Hallowed Realm.

He wants to rule both worlds.

THE CONVERGENCE IS COMING.

The Convergence is the merging of the normal world and the Hallowed Realm.

The players must prevent it.

CONVERGENCE STATUS MONITORING
CONNECTION 0%

Quest 10 is the final confrontation of The Game 2026.

The Hallowed Convergence begins as the Hallow King attempts to complete the connection between the island and the Hallowed Realm.

THE HALLOWED REALM IS HERE.

The players must activate and defend three Severing Anchors while the Hallow King and his forces attempt to stop them.

The battle escalates through multiple phases as the Hallow King, the Killer Bunny and the Crimson Lord enter the confrontation.

Once the Anchors are activated, the players initiate the Severing.

SEVER THE CONNECTION.

The Severing cuts the Hallow King's connection to the world and prevents the complete merging of the two realms.

What remains after the Severing determines what comes next.
